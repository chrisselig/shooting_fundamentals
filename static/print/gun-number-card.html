Your Gun Number

A single personal constant for a mental-math wind hold — built from your own trued dope, not a borrowed rimfire formula.

01

The Formula

Both Directions
Gun Number = (Range ÷ 100) × Wind(mph) × Value ÷ Hold(MIL) Range in yards. Value: 1.0 full (3/9 o'clock), 0.5 half (1/5/7/11), 0 none (12/6).
Hold(MIL) = (Range ÷ 100) × Wind(mph) × Value ÷ Gun Number Same formula, solved for the hold once you know your gun number.
02

Deriving Yours

At Home, Once
  1. Get one trued, full-value, known-speed wind hold at the distance you shoot most (100 yd is typical for NRL22).
  2. Plug range, wind speed, value, and that trued hold into the Gun Number formula above.
  3. Round to one decimal. Write it in the box below.
  4. Check it against a second trued distance — if it's off by more than a click or two there, true that distance on its own rather than trusting one constant past 150–200 yd.

04

Fluctuating FPS

Lot-to-Lot

.22LR velocity swings 30–80 fps lot-to-lot, more in the cold. Slower = more hold. Scale your gun number straight-line off a chrono reading before trusting it past a ~100 fps shift:

Adjusted Gun # = Gun # × (Actual fps ÷ Reference fps)
